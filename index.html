Frequently Asked Questions

Is Wakeup AI really free?

Yes, Wakeup AI is currently completely free to use. We are in early access and want to gather as much feedback as possible to perfect the experience.

How does the AI generate answers?

We use a combination of Deepgram's Nova-2 for ultra-fast transcription and Puter AI's advanced language models to analyze context and generate professional responses.

Is it detectable on Zoom or Teams?

No, Wakeup AI runs as a separate application and does not inject any code into your video conferencing tools. It works via system audio capture.

What platforms are supported?

Wakeup AI is currently available for Windows 10/11 and Android. We are also looking into a macOS version in the future.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the "BYOK" plan?

expand_more

BYOK stands for "Bring Your Own Key". If you have your own Deepgram API key, you can use our software's full capabilities for free. We don't charge you; you only pay Deepgram for what you use (they have a generous free tier too!). This is perfect for developers or those who already have infrastructure API keys.

Can I cancel my Premium subscription?

expand_more

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account dashboard. You will retain access until the end of your billing period. We believe in total flexibility for our users.

Is there an annual billing discount?

expand_more

Absolutely! If you choose yearly billing, you save 20% compared to monthly billing. That's equivalent to getting nearly 2.5 months for free! You can toggle between monthly and yearly rates at the top of this page.

Do I need a credit card for the Demo?

expand_more

No credit card is required to try the Demo. You just need to download the app and sign in with a free Puter account to start your trial sessions immediately.

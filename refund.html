1. Refund Eligibility

We want you to be completely satisfied with Interviewbold. If you are not satisfied with our Premium Copilot service, you may be eligible for a refund under the following conditions:

  • The refund request is made within 7 days of the initial subscription purchase.
  • You have experienced technical issues that prevent you from using the service effectively.
  • You have not exceeded a reasonable amount of AI usage (e.g., more than 3 full interview sessions).

2. Non-Refundable Items

The following items are generally non-refundable:

  • Renewals of existing subscriptions.
  • Subscriptions purchased more than 7 days ago.
  • Promotional or discounted purchases, unless there is a significant technical failure.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email us at support@interviewbold.in with your account details and the reason for your request. We will review your request and notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund within 3-5 business days.

4. Processing Refunds

If your refund is approved, it will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within 7-10 business days.

5. Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation will stop any future billing, but no refunds will be issued for the remaining period of the current billing cycle unless the request meets the 7-day criteria above.
